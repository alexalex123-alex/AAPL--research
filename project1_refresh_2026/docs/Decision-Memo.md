## Why the bullish view and model differ

The student's initiate judgment is preserved as human judgment; it is not substituted for numerical evidence. The model's core range assumes growth converges rapidly after year 5. Alex's thesis may require a much longer economic runway, so the terminal-duration test exposes that requirement rather than merely increasing an unexplained growth rate. Holding the core discount/terminal/bridge assumptions fixed, reverse DCF needs about 19.81% annual FCFF/NOPAT uplift over the already-growing base to match the price. This does not uniquely imply revenue growth.

Microsoft and Alphabet trading prices embed different cloud/AI, advertising, earnings and capital assumptions. Their multiples can anchor a market-relative discussion, but do not resolve Apple's cash conversion or make a long-duration AI thesis a fact. Alphabet's gains and share-class proxy qualify its P/E strongly. No defensible control/synergy transaction reference was supplied.

## Downside, monitoring and reversal

Services regulation, hardware/China replacement weakness and AI-investment overrun produce approximately$117,$118 and$131 references. Combined structural downside is about$61. Cash conservation reduces distributions before violating the operating reserve. These are deterministic stresses, not probabilities.

Alex says disappointing AI features would change his view. Proposed measurable evidence: no incremental upgrade or retention benefit, weak premium mix, Services growth persistently below 6%, Products margin below 35%, or R&D above 12% without cash-flow conversion. These numerical thresholds are analytical proposals, not claimed meeting agreements. Material rate, reinvestment or regulatory changes require a fresh range. High-case initiation needs evidence supporting both strong near-term execution and the unusually long return runway.
